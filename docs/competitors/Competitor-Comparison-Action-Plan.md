## 2. Where We Win, Tie, and Lose

### We win (do not stop talking about these)
- **Free + instant**: our AI Visibility Audit Report, ROI Calculator, Readiness Score, Keyword tool, and 54-prompt library run with zero signup — competitors gate scores behind login or provide only thin free checkers.
- **Done-for-you execution**: no tracker fixes your site — we do (position: "trackers tell you it's broken; we fix it"). AIclicks is the only one blurring into services, and its analytics surface is lighter.
- **Full-service breadth**: 8 service families, ~55 service pages, hosting/dev/marketing/automation under one roof — no SaaS covers this.
- **Own methodology content**: 5-metric rubric + `/how-we-audit-ai-visibility` + llms.txt + benchmark post — citable, differentiating.
- **Email capture now live across all report tools** (ReportGate + OTP sign-in, Aug 2026): free tools = lead engine, matching the category's login-gate model while keeping instant previews.

### We lose / gap (what the plan fixes)
1. **No competitor benchmarking in the free tool.** Every competitor ships "compare vs N competitors" (LLMClicks competitor gap, Otterly benchmarking, Peec competitor bench, OpenLens competitive comparison). Our audit is single-domain.
2. **Pricing on our 5 alternative pages is stale** (LLMClicks LTD pivot, Peec 2026 tiers, Otterly add-ons verified June–Aug 2026).
3. **No plural "alternatives"-style pages** for the new entrants — the "tools like X / best AI visibility tools" surface is now worth 14–22 tools and growing; we own zero of it.
4. **No public benchmark/industry index** from our own audit data (Profound Index, LLMClicks industry benchmarks exist; ours doesn't).
5. **External proof**: no G2 AEO listing, no Product Hunt launches (still open since July plan).
6. **No newsletter/lead lifecycle**: emails now captured via OTP auth — but nothing turns them into campaigns (admin/campaigns exists; capture→list flow unverified).
7. **No agency/partner/white-label page** (retail trackers all have partner programs; we ARE the agency but don't surface it).
8. **Webinar/video** ("is your brand invisible in AI search?") — competitors run these and earn links.

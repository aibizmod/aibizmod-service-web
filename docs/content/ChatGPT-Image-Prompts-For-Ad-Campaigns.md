## CAMPAIGN 1: GOOGLE DISPLAY ADS

### Display Ad Concept 1: "Problem Awareness"
**Dimensions:** 1200 x 628 px (Google Responsive Display)  
**Use Case:** Retargeting website visitors

```
Create a modern, minimalist digital illustration for a display ad about business automation 
inefficiency. Show a frustrated business person (side view, sitting at desk) surrounded by 
floating icons representing repetitive tasks: documents, emails, data entry forms, spreadsheets, 
clocks. The person has an overwhelmed expression. The color palette should be: soft grays 
(#94a3b8), frustrated red accents (#ef4444), and professional blues (#3b82f6). Use clean, 
sans-serif typography overlay at the top reading "Is Your Team Drowning in Manual Work?" 
in bold black text. The style should be professional but accessible, like a modern SaaS 
company. Subtle geometric shapes in the background (circles, lines) for visual interest. 
High contrast, suitable for scrolling feed visibility.
```

### Display Ad Concept 2: "Solution Visualization"
**Dimensions:** 1200 x 628 px  
**Use Case:** Retargeting + brand awareness

```
Create a modern, minimalist visualization of AI automation in action. Show a split-screen 
design: LEFT SIDE = chaos (tangled lines, documents flying everywhere, red warning icons), 
RIGHT SIDE = organized flow (smooth arrows, data flowing smoothly into a funnel, green 
checkmarks). A subtle arrow or connection element bridges the two sides. Center the image 
with a glowing AI/automation symbol (circuit board pattern or nodes connecting). Color 
palette: Deep blue (#1e3a8a) and teal (#06b6d4) gradients, white space, gold/amber accents 
(#f59e0b). Include bold text: "AI Automation Does It Better" at bottom. Modern, tech-forward 
aesthetic. Suitable for Google's audience network.
```

### Display Ad Concept 3: "ROI/Results Focus"
**Dimensions:** 1200 x 628 px  
**Use Case:** Performance/conversion focus

```
Design a clean, data-driven display ad celebrating business success metrics. Feature a 
dashboard-style layout showing upward trending arrows and positive numbers: "25 Hours Saved 
Weekly", "$150K Annual Savings", "6 Weeks to ROI". Use modern chart/graph elements (bars, 
lines) in green (#10b981) against a white background. Include a diverse team (3-4 people) 
celebrating/collaborating in the background (very subtle, 20% opacity). Top text: "Real 
Results from Real Businesses" in professional sans-serif. Bottom CTA area with space for 
"Book Your Free Audit" button. Style: Professional, trustworthy, modern fintech/SaaS 
aesthetic. High readability for thumbnail view.
```

## HOW TO USE THESE PROMPTS

### Step 1: Choose Your Platform & Concept
Pick the campaign type (Google, LinkedIn, Meta) and specific concept number that fits your current need.

### Step 2: Copy the Prompt
Copy the entire prompt text (including the description section).

### Step 3: Paste Into AI Generator
Use one of these tools:
- **ChatGPT DALL-E 3** → Use ChatGPT Plus with GPT-4 and DALL-E integration
- **Midjourney** → Paste into Discord bot
- **Adobe Firefly** → Paste into web interface
- **Microsoft Designer** → Use with Bing Image Creator

### Step 4: Customize
Before generating, you can customize:
- Change company names and stats to match current campaigns
- Adjust color preferences (mention in prompt: "Change purple to brand blue #2563eb")
- Modify text overlays with current headlines/CTAs
- Specify photography style (realistic vs illustrated)

### Step 5: Iterate
If first result doesn't match, try:
- "More vibrant colors" or "More muted, professional tones"
- "Increase focus on [specific element]"
- "Make the text more prominent"
- "Add more people" or "Remove people"

## PRO TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS

### 1. **Combine Prompts**
Mix elements from multiple concepts for unique variations:
```
"Combine Concept 1 (problem visualization) with Concept 3 (ROI stats) 
in a split-screen design optimized for 1080x1080 Instagram format"
```

### 2. **Specify Photo Style**
Add to any prompt:
- "Photography style: modern stock photo aesthetic"
- "Illustration style: minimalist line art"
- "Design style: modern fintech/SaaS"
- "Render style: 3D product visualization"

### 3. **Add Brand Guidelines**
Customize colors:
- "Use brand colors: Primary purple #4f46e5, Secondary blue #3b82f6, Accent green #10b981"
- "Replace all colors with black, white, and gold"

### 4. **Generate Variations**
Run same prompt multiple times with slight variations:
```
First run: "Include team of 4 people"
Second run: "Include team of 2 people"
Third run: "No people, just dashboard and icons"
```

### 5. **A/B Testing**
Generate 2-3 variations per concept:
- Version A: Photograph style
- Version B: Illustration style
- Version C: Minimalist/text-focused

Test each in your platform to see what performs best.

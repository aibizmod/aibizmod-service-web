## 2. The Six Functional Platform Pillars

All marketing initiatives must align with at least one of the following 6 pillars:

1. **Lead Capture & Conversion Engine (`ReportGate`)**: Free interactive tools gate full reports behind OTP email sign-in. All marketing must drive users to unlock reports, followed by automated 4-touch technical nurture drips that convert to booked consultations.
2. **Core Services & Subservices (61+ Pages)**: Positioning high-margin capabilities (Agentic AI, LLM fine-tuning, process automation, cloud infrastructure, custom software) for search engines and generative AI answer engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Google AI Overviews).
3. **Interactive Tool Distribution**: Promoting our free proprietary tools (`/ai-visibility-audit-report`, `/automation-roi-calculator`, `/keywords`, `/llms-txt-generator`) as top-of-funnel magnets.
4. **Competitor Comparison & Market Surveillance**: Maintaining accurate build-vs-buy and alternative pages against competitors (LLMClicks, Peec, Profound, Otterly, AIClicks) with current 2026 pricing and capability matrices.
5. **Technical Thought Leadership (Blog & Topics)**: Publishing architectural deep-dives with zero AI fluff, verified stats, and rich schema markup.
6. **20 Industry Verticals (`/industries/*`)**: Delivering sector-specific architectural case studies and compliance frameworks (Healthcare, FinTech, Retail, etc.).

## 4. Quantitative & Compliance Guardrails

* **Deterministic Claims Verification:** Never promise unsubstantiated numbers. Every metric (latency, uptime, ROI multiplier) must be verified against actual architectural baselines or client case studies.
* **Compliance Standards:**
  - **Healthcare:** HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) readiness, private VPC isolation, zero PHI model exposure.
  - **Financial Services:** SOC2 Type II controls, automated transaction audit trails, zero data retention on public AI models.
  - **European Union:** Full compliance with GDPR and EU AI Act Article 50 (C2PA content provenance signing on AI visual assets, deepfake and AI-generation disclosures).
  - **India:** Full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).
